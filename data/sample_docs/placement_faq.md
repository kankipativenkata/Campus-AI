# Placement FAQ (FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA)

**Who is eligible for campus placements?**
Final-year students with a minimum CGPA of 7.0 and no active backlogs are eligible.

**How many offers can a student hold?**
A student may hold one offer under the "one student, one offer" policy. A student may sit for a "dream company" drive only if the offered package is at least 1.5 times the existing offer.

**How do I register for a drive?**
Register on the placement portal at least 48 hours before the drive date. Late registrations are not accepted.

**What is the dress code for drives?**
Formal attire is mandatory for all pre-placement talks, tests and interviews.

**Can I skip classes for a drive?**
Students attending a drive receive on-duty attendance if they submit the company confirmation mail to the placement office.
