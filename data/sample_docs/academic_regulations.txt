DEMO UNIVERSITY - ACADEMIC REGULATIONS (FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA)
Version 2.0, effective 1 July 2026

1. Attendance
A student must have a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to write the end-semester examination.
Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation of attendance shortage. A valid medical certificate or an approved participation letter is required. A condonation fee of Rs. 500 per course is charged.
Students with attendance below 65% are detained in that course and must repeat it in the next offering.

2. Grading
Grades are awarded on a 10-point scale. Grade S: 90 to 100 marks (10 grade points). Grade A: 80 to 89 marks (9 grade points). Grade B: 70 to 79 marks (8 grade points). Grade C: 60 to 69 marks (7 grade points). Grade D: 50 to 59 marks (6 grade points). Grade E: 40 to 49 marks (5 grade points). Below 40 marks is Grade F (fail).

3. Re-evaluation
A student may apply for re-evaluation of an end-semester answer script within 7 days of the publication of results. The re-evaluation fee is Rs. 1000 per course. The revised marks, if different, replace the original marks.

4. Continuous Assessment
Each course has continuous assessment worth 60 marks (two Continuous Assessment Tests, quizzes and assignments) and the end-semester examination worth 40 marks. A student must score at least 40% in the end-semester examination to pass the course.

5. Academic Integrity
Plagiarism in assignments, lab records or examinations is treated as malpractice. The first offence results in zero marks for that component. A second offence results in a failing grade for the course.
