# Demo University Hostel Rules (FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA)
Version 1.4, effective 1 August 2026

## Gate timings
Hostel gates close at 9:30 PM for all residents. Late entry after 9:30 PM needs written permission from the warden.

## Visitors
Parents and guardians may visit on Sundays between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Visitors must register at the hostel office and carry a photo ID. Visitors are not allowed inside rooms.

## Mess timings
Breakfast is served from 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM. Lunch is from 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM. Snacks are from 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM. Dinner is from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM.

## Room change
Room change requests can be submitted to the warden only during the first two weeks of a semester. Requests are approved subject to availability.

## Wi-Fi and electricity
Wi-Fi is available in all rooms. Use of personal heaters, induction stoves and immersion rods is strictly prohibited. Violations attract a fine of Rs. 2000.

## Laundry
Laundry service is available on Tuesdays and Fridays. Each student gets 15 clothes per week free of charge.
