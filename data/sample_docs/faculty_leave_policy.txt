FACULTY LEAVE POLICY (FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA, FACULTY ONLY)

Casual leave: Faculty members are entitled to 12 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave cannot be combined with vacation for more than 3 consecutive working days.
Earned leave: 30 days per year for faculty on 12-month contracts.
Duty leave: Granted for conferences and workshops with prior approval from the Head of Department and Dean, at least 7 days in advance.
Evaluation duty: Faculty must submit evaluated answer scripts within 5 working days of the examination.
